# Which Tool For What

*Updated July 27, 2026. Was a 3-week bake-off; collapsed after Max called two of the three
categories correctly up front.*

---

## Settled

**Graphics → Cowork.** It's the only one with image generation and Canva. Not close.

**X recap, and anything recurring → Cowork.** The only one that can see your live X account and
the only one that can run on a schedule. The weekly `VOICE.md` update already runs here every
Monday at 9am.

**Site and code changes → Codex or Claude Code, interchangeably.** Both are good at this. You
won't be doing enough of it for the difference to matter. Use whichever is already open.

That's the whole decision. No test needed.

---

## The one thing still worth paying attention to

Not *which tool*, but **whether either one can write in your voice** once it's read `VOICE.md`.
That's genuinely unknown and it's the thing that's failed twice already.

No formal test. Just notice, whenever you ask any tool for post copy:

- How many rounds of "no, not like that" before it's usable?
- Did it produce something you'd actually post, or something you had to rewrite into your own words?

**"Rounds of correction" is the metric.** Time is noisy. How often you had to correct it is the
real signal.

If a tool consistently needs three-plus rounds even with `VOICE.md` in front of it, stop asking it
for copy and use it only for production work. That's a legitimate outcome — drafting in your own
voice is the hardest thing to delegate and the least important to.

## Division of labor

**You decide:** the rankings, the real reason for controversial placements, which jokes land, what
gets posted.

**AI prepares:** caption options, graphics, the weekly recap, riser/faller candidates, "I Was
Wrong" candidates from real saved takes.

**The site produces:** exact weekly graphics from published data, the archive, permanent links.

---

## The one rule

**Nothing posts automatically.** Any of these can draft, render, and prepare. You read and send
every public word. The account's only real advantage is that a specific person with taste is
behind it — automating approval would delete the thing that makes it work.
